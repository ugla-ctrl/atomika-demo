## Historical reference

The Atlas Missile Museum of Texas restored an original launch control console acquired from an Atlas F site in Nebraska. Its restoration page includes a demonstration and close-up instrument photograph:

https://www.atlasmissilemuseumoftexas.org/completed-projects

Demonstration: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RlYZZxaZyco

This establishes the form of the main console: a low, wide operator desk, angled indicator panels, switches and analog gauges. It does not establish the exact layout of the Atomika room.
